# Next tests

The next physical step is to supply information these diagnostics intentionally
hold fixed, rather than tune the finite scans until they pass a desired gate.

1. Match the Wilson holonomy kinetic action with a stated compactification,
   spectrum, boundary conditions, renormalization convention, counterterms and
   threshold terms. Test whether the allowed metric actually has the restricted
   charge-correlated structure used here. Keep gauge-vector and A5 actions
   distinct until their relation is derived.
2. Construct the finite-temperature potential analytically at the origin and
   through symmetry restoration, then determine whether the fixed source used
   in the duration diagnostic exists over the relevant interval.
3. Derive a physical CP-odd invariant, source/operator and autonomous reservoir.
   Evolve the full energy exchange and shutdown; an externally prescribed pulse
   or chosen initial sign is insufficient.
4. Derive and solve the response with sphaleron rates, washout, spectators and
   changing entropy. Compute the final signed abundance. Replace the fixed-u
   duration condition with the actual transport weighting; the present bound
   should not be imposed on every possible yield history.
5. Recompute total energy, relics, physical vacuum identifications and defects
   in the constructed branch. Do not import the old scalar model's matter or
   anomaly sector automatically into the Wilson alternative.

Recover and inspect the older private transport work for technical ideas before
reimplementing it, but independently validate its assumptions against the current
branch. Keep raw private files private. Check current primary literature and
experimental constraints when network access becomes available. A source-only
trajectory or necessary winding bound is not a substitute for those steps.
