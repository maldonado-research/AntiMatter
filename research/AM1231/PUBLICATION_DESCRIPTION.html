In More Basic Terms

This project asks whether a chain of interacting fields could help explain why matter survived over antimatter. One proposed mathematical route fails a symmetry test. A separate extra-dimensional construction remains possible under stated assumptions, but its energy supply, particle interactions and early-Universe history are unfinished. The new calculation shows that even modest inefficiency makes the required field motion harder to supply. This is progress in testing the idea and identifying its next falsifiable steps, not a claim that the problem has been solved.
